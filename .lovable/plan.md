# Compact mobile category links and CTAs

## Goal
Reduce the mobile footprint of homepage category cards and regular homepage CTA links while preserving imagery, destinations, behavior, and all catalogue-grid work.

## Changes
- Keep homepage categories at two columns from 360px through mobile widths, with one column only below 360px.
- Give category images a compact 4:5 frame, smaller mobile padding, and contained images without distortion.
- Clamp centered category names to two lines and hide descriptions on mobile to keep multiple cards visible at once; retain descriptions on tablet and desktop.
- Reduce homepage mobile section spacing and mobile CTA margins/padding while retaining at least a 44px tap height and fit-content width.
- Preserve current tablet/desktop category layouts, hover behavior, URLs, and product catalogue/card styling.

## Validation
- Check the homepage at 320px, 375px, 390px, 430px, and desktop.
- Confirm column counts, tap sizes, image proportions, text clamping, no horizontal overflow, and unchanged navigation targets.
